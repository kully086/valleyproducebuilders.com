🪴 Garden Care

We take care of the work so your garden stays healthy, productive, and looking its best throughout the growing season.

💧 Regular Watering

We keep your garden properly watered throughout the growing season. Watering is adjusted based on the weather, soil, irrigation system, and plants you're growing. During hot weather, gardens may typically need watering 3–4 times per week, with deeper watering when needed.

🌿 Weeding & Garden Cleanup

We regularly remove weeds before they take over and keep your garden beds clean, organized, and productive.

🌱 Plant Feeding

We monitor your plants and add fertilizer, compost, manure, or other soil amendments when needed to help keep your garden growing strong.

🐛 Pest & Plant Checks

We inspect your plants during regular visits for signs of pests, disease, or other problems and address issues before they become bigger problems.

✂️ Pruning & Maintenance

We remove dead or damaged growth, maintain plants as they grow, and keep your garden organized and easy to access.

🍅 Harvesting

When your vegetables and fruit are ready, you choose how you'd like us to handle the harvest. You can pick your own produce, or we can harvest it for you during your regular garden-care visit. Either way, we'll help keep your plants producing and make sure ripe produce doesn't go to waste.

☀️ Seasonal Care

As the seasons change, we adjust your garden-care routine, replace finished plants, prepare beds for new crops, and keep your garden producing throughout the year.
